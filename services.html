Our services

Hearing care, start to finish.

From your first hearing test to follow-up after fitting, everything is looked after in one place.

What we do

Care for every step.

01

Hearing Aid Assessment

A check of how you hear today, to find out whether a hearing aid could help.

02

Hearing Aid Consultation

Talk through your options, the styles and brands, and what to expect.

03

Hearing Aid Fitting & Verification

Your hearing aids are fitted and set up for you, then checked to make sure they work as they should.

04

Hearing Aid Repair & Maintenance

Cleaning, checks and repairs to keep your hearing aids working well.

05

Tinnitus Evaluation & Management

Support if you hear ringing, buzzing or other sounds that are not there.

06

Vestibular Assessment & Rehabilitation

Assessment and rehabilitation if you feel dizzy or unsteady.

07

Counselling & Follow-up

Time to ask questions and get comfortable, with follow-up after your fitting.

Hearing aid styles

Find the style that fits you.

BTE

Behind the Ear

The device sits behind the ear and carries sound into the ear.

RIC

Receiver in the Canal

A small unit sits behind the ear, with the speaker placed in the ear canal.

CIC

Completely in the Canal

A small, custom-fit device that sits inside the ear canal.

IIC

Invisible in the Canal

The smallest style, worn deep in the ear canal so it is barely visible.

Which style suits you depends on your hearing, your ears and your daily life. We will talk it through with you after your assessment.
