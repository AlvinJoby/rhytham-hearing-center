Our story

Listening comes first.

Rhytham is a speech, hearing and balance centre in Kozhippilly, Kothamangalam, where every visit begins with a conversation.

Who we are

A conversation before anything else.

Good hearing keeps us close to the people who matter most.

Tell us what you have noticed, and we will help you work out what to do next. Whether it is a hearing test, a conversation about hearing aids, or support for ringing in the ears or balance, you will find it under one roof.

We take the time to listen first, because the right care always begins with understanding what matters to you.

Talk to our team →

What we care for

Speech, hearing and balance.

Three things that quietly shape everyday life, looked after in one place.

01

Hearing

Hearing tests, hearing aid assessment and consultation, fitting and verification, and repair and maintenance.

02

Balance

Vestibular assessment and rehabilitation for people who feel dizzy or unsteady, so everyday movement feels sure again.

03

Speech

Hearing and speaking go hand in hand. Tell us which conversations have become harder, and we will help you find the right next step.

How we look after you

One step at a time, together.

  1. Listen

    You tell us what you have noticed and what you would like to hear better.

  2. Assess

    A careful assessment and consultation to understand your hearing and your options.

  3. Fit

    Hearing aids are fitted for you and verified, so they work the way they should.

  4. Follow up

    Counselling, follow-up, repair and maintenance, long after the first visit.
